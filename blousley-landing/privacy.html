Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

1. What data we collect

Blousley may collect information you provide directly, such as your name, email address, design details, images, and any messages you send to tailors or support.

2. How we use your data

We use your data to operate the Blousley marketplace, connect you with tailors, improve our services, and communicate with you about your account or orders.

3. Sharing your data

We share information with tailors as needed to fulfill your requests. We do not sell your personal data to third parties.

4. Data retention and deletion

You can request deletion of your account and associated data by contacting us at hello@blousley.com.

5. Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, contact us at hello@blousley.com.
